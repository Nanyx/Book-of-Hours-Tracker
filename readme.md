# Book of Hours Tracker

A companion app to use alongside of your playtime of **[Book of Hours](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1028310/BOOK_OF_HOURS/)** by _[Weather Factory](https://weatherfactory.biz/)_. This app is by no mean there to "play" the game for you or give you hints. It simply replace the usage of a massive pile of notes or complicated spreadsheet. To keep the obtuse nature of the game alive while using this app the interface will not be explain, but be assure your knowledge acquired in the game will let you master the application.

## V1

The v1 of the app require an hands-on approach of your current game state. The app will need frequent input from your part to be updated and you'll have to manually manage saves between each new game playthrough.

## V2 (alpha)

The v2 hooks itself to your current **autosave** game file, removing any input from your end. The autosave frequency can be adjust in the settings of the game. The tracker will refresh automatically.

## V3 (planned)

A v3 is planned (not yet in development) to use a **mod dll** to watch over changes in relevant game state data and update the app, removing delay between action in-game and app-state.
